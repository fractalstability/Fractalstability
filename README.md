## AggieAir @ USU

[AggieAir](https://uwrl.usu.edu/aggieair/) is USU's small UAS program for scientific remote sensing. After 10 years as Director, I serve as the leader of R&D, overseeing platform development, avionics, payloads, and flight operations. Our fleet supports water resources research, agricultural monitoring, and environmental science across the Intermountain West.

The platform was built on the open-source [Paparazzi autopilot](https://github.com/paparazzi/paparazzi), [RT-Paparazzi](https://github.com/AggieAir/fixedwing-rt) and has been expanded with custom avionics, sensor suites, and multi-UAV coordination capability, and now is a proud supporter and official Partner of the [ArduPilot](https://github.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot) project.
